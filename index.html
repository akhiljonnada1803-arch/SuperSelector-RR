How to read the board

Definitions and data limitations
Metrics, not mystery scores
  • Batting average = runs ÷ recorded dismissals. Blank when no dismissal is recorded.
  • Strike rate = runs ÷ balls faced × 100. Wides excluded from balls faced.
  • Economy = recorded runs ÷ (bowling balls ÷ 6). The supplied extras1 field is inconsistent (it is 6 on ordinary deliveries), so extras are excluded and economy is an estimate.
  • Quality percentile is a within-role rank (0–100) built from percentile ranks of runs, average, strike rate and wickets/economy. Batting and bowling inputs require at least 60 recorded balls. It is a comparison aid, not a forecast.
  • Role labels are inferred from recorded batting positions and bowling workload. Sample size varies; check balls, matches and dismissals before comparing.
  • RR history flags players listed for Rajasthan Royals in the mapping data. It does not imply current contract or recruitment recommendation. The pressure shortlist and displayed figures follow Report.pdf, which analyzes IPL and T20I together for 2022–2026. Its pressure definition is death overs 16–20 or four or more wickets lost. The batter qualification is 150 pressure balls; the bowler qualification is 400 legal pressure balls. Phil Salt, Mitchell Starc, Jamie Overton and Abrar Ahmed are retained below threshold as scouting or impact exceptions. The report order is for presentation, not ranking, and no composite score is used. These files do not provide role targets, player availability, salary or squad constraints, so the dashboard avoids inventing an RR recruitment-fit score.
